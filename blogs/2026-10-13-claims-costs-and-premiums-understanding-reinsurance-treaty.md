Reinsurance Explained Claims, Costs, and Premiums: Understanding Reinsurance Treaty Performance Reinsurance, like any other area of finance, is a for-profit business. Reinsurers carefully select, price, and negotiate treaties with an eye toward one primary goal: fulfilling their obligations to the insurer while maximizing margins. For the average reinsurer, that means greater value for shareholders.
