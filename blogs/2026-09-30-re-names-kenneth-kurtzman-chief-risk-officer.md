Team Re Names Kenneth Kurtzman Chief Risk Officer Re is excited to welcome Kenneth Kurtzman as chief risk officer. Ken brings more than 35 years of experience across actuarial work, underwriting, and risk management. He joins Re from an independent consulting practice, where he advised insurers on reserving, reinsurance, and business strategy.
