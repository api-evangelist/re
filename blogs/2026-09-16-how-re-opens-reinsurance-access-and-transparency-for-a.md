Access & Transparency How Re Opens Reinsurance, Access and Transparency for a Closed Market When you buy insurance, it’s something you can see: you purchase a policy, and if anything goes wrong that’s covered by that policy, you file a claim. Behind that policy, however, sits another layer you’ll never see: reinsurance. Reinsurance is effectively a hidden layer in more ways than that.
